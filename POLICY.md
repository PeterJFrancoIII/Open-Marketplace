# Default marketplace policy

An open marketplace does not mean unlawful exchange. A public instance needs a
short, predictable policy and an appeal path. `Master_Descriptor.md` defines the
default product direction; paid or host priority never overrides this policy.

## Restricted listings

This starter rejects listings for:

- firearms, ammunition, explosives, silencers, and weapon-conversion parts;
- controlled substances, drug paraphernalia, and unsafe medical products;
- stolen goods, counterfeit goods, and items that infringe intellectual property;
- human exploitation, sexual services, or sexual content involving minors;
- live animals where prohibited, endangered species, and protected wildlife products;
- personal data, account credentials, malware, or tools primarily intended for unauthorized access;
- recalled products and anything else unlawful in the seller's or buyer's jurisdiction.

A restricted, fraudulent, quarantined, or otherwise ineligible listing cannot
buy or retain priority. A payment is not an endorsement and does not reduce the
seller's obligations.

## Host verification and identity privacy

High-assurance identity verification applies to users seeking Verified Host
benefits, not automatically to every regular account. The enrollment notice
must explain the checks, purpose, processor, retention, possible disclosures,
and appeal options before evidence is collected.

Only the designated verification provider or tightly controlled trust function
may process raw government ID, document numbers, selfies, biometric templates,
or corroborating address evidence. That source evidence must never appear in a
listing, public profile, marketplace registry, decentralized replica, analytics
event, or application log. Marketplace and community-host systems receive only
the minimal signed, revocable attestation described in `Master_Descriptor.md`.

Any confidential mapping used to identify a host after credible fraud or misuse
must be encrypted, access-controlled, and disclosed only under a published,
documented investigation process or valid legal process. Every access is
audited. Verification status must not be used to dox a host publicly.

Operators must provide correction, expiration, revocation, deletion, breach
response, accessibility, and appeal procedures appropriate to the selected
verification provider and jurisdictions. Collect no more data and retain it no
longer than the approved purpose requires.

## Priority, advertising, and tracking

Priority treatment must be visibly labeled **Verified Host Priority** or **Paid
Priority**, use the published ranking rules, and remain subject to moderation.
Hidden administrator boosts and individually negotiated ordering are not part of
the default policy.

Verified Host mode is ad-free and disables nonessential advertising,
cross-site-tracking, and optional analytics identifiers. Strictly necessary
authentication, security, fraud-prevention, and requested preference storage
must be documented separately and may not be repurposed for advertising.

## Operator duties

Instance operators should publish local additions, document enforcement and
ranking decisions, provide report and appeal routes, disclose material funding
and paid placement, and avoid collecting more user data than enforcement
actually needs. This policy is a technical default, not legal advice.
